About Kaviscape

A new way to experience music.

KAVISCAPE is an early-stage project exploring new possibilities for interactive music experiences. Our vision is to bring listening, learning, practice and performance closer together through technology. We are developing and validating the concept, with the goal of creating more personal, flexible and creative ways for people to engage with music.
